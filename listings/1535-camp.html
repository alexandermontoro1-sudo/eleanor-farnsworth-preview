About this property

This townhouse, built in 1858 and renovated in 2023, located in a unique park-like setting one-half block from New Orleans' historic Coliseum Square boasts its original "shrimpy pink" facade and features classic examples of the city's architecture. The design thoughtfully integrates the surrounding green trees, visible through the windows, and the rear courtyard has been transformed into a cool oasis with fountain, designed by noted landscape architect Ryan Cole.

Balcony overlooks the park. Original chandeliers, marble mantels, wood floors, 13 foot ceilings, elevator, new HVAC units, lots of natural light. Main level kitchen features custom stainless cabinetry, Soapstone countertops, SubZero Refrigerator/Freezer and Wolf Range. Reclaimed 18th century marble adorn the kitchen and bath floors, Lefroy Brooks plumbing fixtures.

Currently set up as 2 units, but easily convertible back to single family use. Easy access to CBD, Uptown, Magazine Street shops and restaurants.
