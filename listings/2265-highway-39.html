About this property

Completely renovated, secluded 3,968 sq. ft .home has 5 bedrooms, 4 full baths, 2 half baths and sits one-half mile off of Highway 39 on 84.75 acres. Great for weekend getaways, hunting, or just enjoying the outdoors. The primary suite has large sitting area, 2 full baths, bedroom with vaulted ceiling and kitchenette.

The living room has a fireplace, vaulted ceilings and overlooks the back patio. The kitchen is well appointed with granite counters, stainless appliances, soft close cabinets and drawers. A 1 bedroom, 1 bath apartment with separate entrance is included in the total area. Located just 5 minutes from the Belle Chasse Ferry and 40 minutes from downtown New Orleans.

Thinking about investment or developing? Seller has 34 one acre lots plotted on the land between the highway and the house.
