1.29 acres overlooking Cypress Lakes Golf Course.

Nestled within the sought-after Ormond Estates, this exceptional property spans 1.29 acres and commands amazing views of the Cypress Lakes Golf Course.

The home was completely renovated in 2021. A grand foyer opens to the living room, an expansive dining area and a den with floor-to-ceiling windows, a fireplace, a full bar and views out to the course. The kitchen offers a large island and stainless steel appliances, and the main level is finished in chandeliers, marble, wood and brick flooring.

The primary suite sits on the first floor with dual walk-in closets, two bathrooms and direct access to the pool and patio overlooking the golf course.

Outdoors there is a pool, a patio with covered seating, a natural gas grill and a separate guest house and cabana with two full bathrooms and a kitchen. Landscaping, a circular driveway, abundant parking and a two-car garage complete the property. This one-of-a-kind masterpiece presents unparalleled vistas of the Cypress Lakes Country Club's emerald fairways and manicured greens.
