On the Avenue, across from the Orleans Club.

An elegant home across from the Orleans Club and steps from the Latter Library, in a prime location on historic St. Charles Avenue.

The plan flows well for entertaining. A large dining room opens to a sitting room whose curved wall of windows gives fantastic views of the passing streetcars and the Avenue itself. The gourmet kitchen opens to a spacious, light-filled den with a working fireplace, which in turn overlooks a charming private fenced patio.

The first-floor primary suite is generous, with a large bath and walk-in closet. A beautiful winding staircase leads to four bedrooms and two baths upstairs. A long driveway leads to an attached two-car garage.

The location is genuinely walkable, close to Crepe Nanou, the JCC, Zara's, coffee shops, an ice cream parlor and the shops along the Avenue.
