About this property

This totally renovated, elegant condo is an exceptional buy. Boasts luxurious touches throughout, marble floors, crown molding, gold-accent hardware. Features include French doors in each room, marble fireplace, built-in bookcases, and custom millwork. The kitchen is fully equipped with Bosch appliances and glass-front cabinets.

The large primary bedroom suite offers abundant closet space, and the en-suite bath is a luxurious retreat with heated floors, a spacious walk-in shower with a bench, and elegant Sherrill Wagner fixtures. Included in the condo fee are electric, water, basic cable, building insurance, a termite contract, and 24-hour security.

Property management and engineers are also on the premises 24 hours a day. Building amenities include a fitness gym, hair salon, library, swimming pool, valet parking, laundry room, and an onsite restaurant.
